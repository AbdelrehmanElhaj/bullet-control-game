Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Bullet Control ("the App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

Bullet Control does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App operates entirely offline on your device.

  • No account or registration is required.
  • No personal data is collected.
  • No location data is accessed.
  • No camera, microphone, or contacts are accessed.

2. Internet Access

Bullet Control does not require an internet connection to function. The App does not make any network requests and does not communicate with any external servers.

3. Data Storage

Any game data (such as scores or settings) is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by us or any third party.

4. Third-Party Services

Bullet Control does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or social media platforms.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since we collect no data at all, the App is safe for all ages.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
